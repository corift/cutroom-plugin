# Caption: serif

`"caption": "serif"` — a bold white sans phrase with its keywords in a big gold italic serif (New York Italic). The whole line blurs in and rises 20px.

- Needs keywords: set `keys` on the plan or the section. One or two per phrase. Without them it's a plain small line.
- Pick nouns and verbs that carry the claim: "design file", "every single", "remembers".
- Sits at y 1460. Use it in the calmer middle sections.
- Code: `V.serif_caps`.
