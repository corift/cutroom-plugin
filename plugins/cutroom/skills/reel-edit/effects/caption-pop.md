# Caption: pop

`"caption": "pop"` — heavy white caps (SF Pro Black 96) with a thick black edge, three words at a time. Each word pops in with a small overshoot as it's said; the word being said is gold.

- The default style and the one to open and close on.
- Sits at y 1470. Shrinks to fit 940px, so long words are fine.
- Phrases break at punctuation, pauses over 0.35s, and every 3 words.
- Code: `V.pop_caps`.
