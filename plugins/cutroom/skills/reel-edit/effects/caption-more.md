# Caption: the other styles

Seven more, each from one creator in the study. Use them as `"caption"` on a section. They sit at y 1250 unless the section sets `cy`.

| style | look | keys |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `karaoke` | heavy white caps, a red box jumps to the word being said | no |
| `boxed` | white phrase, the keyword black on a tilted yellow box | yes |
| `one_word` | one giant word at a time; keywords slam in bigger and yellow | yes |
| `pill` | a plain sentence on a dark translucent pill, hard swaps | no |
| `stack2x` | the phrase, then its keyword on its own line at 2x | yes |
| `serif_key` | bold sans, the keyword in an orange italic serif | yes |
| `grey_next` | the whole phrase up; said words white, upcoming ones grey | no |

Code: `V.CAPTIONS`.
