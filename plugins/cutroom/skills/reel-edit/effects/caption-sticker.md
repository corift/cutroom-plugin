# Caption: sticker

`"caption": "sticker"` — one word at a time, lower case, black on a white rounded sticker that tilts -3° then +3° with each word and pops in.

- For quick lists and fast talking. Short sections only (2–4s): it gets tiring.
- Sits low (y 1800) so it clears the chin. Check it when the face is low in frame.
- Tilt is allowed in this look only.
- Code: `V.sticker_caps`.
