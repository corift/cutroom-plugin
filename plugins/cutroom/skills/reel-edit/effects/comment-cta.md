# Comment CTA

A comment field rises from the bottom; on the keyword it types the word, Post lights up, and the word lands huge in gold behind the head.

```json
{"do": "comment", "at": "comment", "word": "EDIT", "word_at": "edit"}
```

- The last section. Hold it at least 1.5s after the word.
- Whoosh on the bar, impact and a small shake on the word. Captions hide from the word on.
- `sub` ("comment") is the serif line above the word.
- Code: `V.comment_bar`, `V.big_word`.
